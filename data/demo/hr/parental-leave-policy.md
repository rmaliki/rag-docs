# Acme Corp Parental Leave Policy

Effective 1 January 2026. Applies to all permanent employees of Acme Corp.

## Entitlement

Every employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption or surrogacy is entitled to 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The entitlement is the same for all parents, regardless of gender.

Employees with less than six months of service receive 10 weeks of fully paid leave, followed by up to 10 weeks of unpaid leave.

## Taking leave

Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into up to three blocks within the first 18 months after the child arrives. Employees should notify their manager and HR at least eight weeks before the first block starts.

## Returning to work

Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced schedule of 80 percent at full pay for the first four weeks after their return. Their role, grade and salary are protected during the leave.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the HR team through the employee service portal.
