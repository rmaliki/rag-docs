# Acme Corp Executive Compensation 2026

Restricted. Distribution limited to HR and the executive committee.

## Base salaries

The executive salaries approved by the remuneration committee for 2026 are:

- Chief Executive Officer: base salary of 420,000 EUR.
- Chief Financial Officer: base salary of 310,000 EUR.
- Chief Marketing Officer: base salary of 265,000 EUR.
- Chief People Officer: base salary of 240,000 EUR.

## Variable pay

Executives have an annual bonus target of 40 percent of base salary, paid between 0 and 150 percent of target depending on company revenue growth and individual objectives.

## Long-term incentives

Each executive receives a long-term incentive grant equal to 60 percent of base salary, vesting over three years.
