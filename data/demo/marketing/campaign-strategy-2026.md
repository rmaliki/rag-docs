# Northern Lights Campaign Strategy

Marketing team internal document. Second half of 2026.

## Objective

Northern Lights is our latest campaign. Its goal is to grow awareness of Acme Corp's cloud analytics product among mid-sized manufacturers in the Nordics and Benelux, and to generate 1,200 qualified leads by the end of the year.

## Audience and message

The primary audience is operations directors at manufacturers with 200 to 2,000 employees. The core message is that Acme turns factory data into decisions in days, not months.

## Channels

The campaign strategy combines LinkedIn sponsored content, a webinar series with customer case studies, and presence at two industry trade fairs. Paid search supports the launch weeks.

## Measurement

Success is measured by qualified leads, webinar attendance and pipeline generated. Results are reviewed every two weeks with sales leadership.
