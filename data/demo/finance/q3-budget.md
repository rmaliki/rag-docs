# Acme Corp Q3 2026 Budget

Confidential. Finance team and executive committee only.

## Summary

The approved Q3 2026 operating budget is 18.4 million EUR, a 6 percent increase on Q2.

## Allocation by department

- Engineering and product: 7.9 million EUR.
- Sales: 4.1 million EUR.
- Marketing: 3.2 million EUR, including 1.1 million EUR for the Northern Lights campaign.
- Human Resources: 1.4 million EUR.
- Finance and administration: 1.8 million EUR.

## Controls

Any spend above 50,000 EUR outside the approved allocation requires CFO approval. Budgets are reviewed monthly against actuals and re-forecast at the end of each month.
